Community Sizing: A Human-Scale Approach

Our plan for growth is designed around a simple truth: people thrive in communities where they feel they belong. We prioritize social health over simple expansion, using a "network of villages" model to balance intimacy with diversity.

Phase 1: The Pioneer Group (30-50 Members)

The first community begins with a small, dedicated group. This size is large enough to have a variety of skills needed to build the first sustainable systems, but small enough that everyone can form deep, trusting relationships. To ensure the foundation is strong, all pioneers are chosen through a Values-Alignment Questionnaire that selects for people who are open-minded and committed to our core principles.

Phase 2: The Stable Village (100-150 Members)

Research shows that people can maintain about 150 stable relationships. This is our target for a healthy, self-sustaining community. At this size, there is a rich social life and diverse set of skills, but it's still possible to know everyone. To keep things fair and organized, formal systems like our community voting (DAO) and conflict resolution processes become essential.

Phase 3: The Network of Villages (Scaling Out, Not Up)

To grow bigger without losing what makes us special, we will not build massive cities. Instead, we will help start new, independent communities. Imagine a network of many small, thriving villages connected by shared values and technology. This model offers the best of both worlds: the trust and closeness of a small village, with the diversity and opportunity of a global network.

Travel & Passport System

To ensure our network of villages feels like one large, interconnected family, we've designed a system to make travel between them effortless, equitable, and a celebrated part of our culture.

Travel Coins: A Special-Purpose Token

Think of these like airline miles, not money. Every member receives an annual allotment of Travel Coins. These special-purpose tokens can only be used to book stays at other communities and cannot be bought or sold. The "price" of a stay is dynamic, calculated by an automated program (a smart contract) based on demand, which encourages members to explore all corners of our network.

Flexible Rewards for Contributions

When you complete a task for the community (a "bounty"), you can often choose your reward: our community currency (LNX) or Travel Coins. To account for life's changes, you can switch your preference anytime before the task is finished, ensuring the reward you receive is the one you need most at that moment.

"Sponsored Journeys": The Gift of Travel

A member can use their own Travel Coins to gift a trip to a friend or loved one. This creates a special, one-time "ticket" (a non-transferable token, or SBT) that only the recipient can use. This is a pure act of kindness. If the recipient can't take the trip for any reason—or even in the sad event of their passing—the Travel Coins are automatically and fully returned to the giver. This ensures a gift never becomes a burden or a source of resentment.

Fairness for Maturing Coins

Travel Coins are meant to be used. After a set time (e.g., 18 months), they "mature." To encourage active participation, members who have traveled within that time get a final choice: book one last trip or convert the coins to LNX. If a member hasn't traveled, their maturing coins are contributed to a "Community Travel Pool" that helps fund long-distance travel for all members. This ensures every contribution benefits the network.

Passport Stamps: Digital Souvenirs

When you visit a new community, you can get a unique digital "stamp" by tapping your device on a terminal (an NFC or QR code interaction). This stamp is a personal, non-tradable digital collectible (a Soulbound Token or SBT) that celebrates your travels and shows your experience within our global network.

Transport & Community Outreach

Our community-owned fleet of self-driving electric vehicles is more than a convenience; it's a bridge to our neighbors and a source of shared prosperity.

A Fleet with Two Missions

The primary role of our EV fleet (a DAO-owned asset) is to provide seamless travel between our communities for members, with the cost included when they use their Travel Coins. Its second mission is to serve the public. The fleet operates as an affordable, eco-friendly ride-share service for surrounding towns, generating income that is sent directly to the community treasury.

The "Community Lift" Program

We believe in being good neighbors. A portion of our fleet's time is dedicated to providing free, essential rides for people in need in the surrounding areas. We will partner with local charities and social services to make this happen, demonstrating our commitment to the well-being of the entire region.

Shared Technology, Fair Rewards

Our self-driving technology will be based on open-source projects (like Comma.ai's Openpilot), creating learning opportunities for our own members. The necessary work of cleaning and maintaining the vehicles will be posted as tasks ("bounties") on our community hub. The rewards for these tasks will be scaled fairly based on the effort required—a simple cleaning is different from sanitizing a vehicle after a medical transport, and the compensation will reflect that.

Marketplace & A Fair Economy

Our economy is designed to provide for our members first, while creating a healthy and inviting relationship with the outside world.

Tier 1: Member Access (A Basic Right)

Essential goods produced by the community, like fresh food, are considered a basic right. Members can acquire these items for a nominal fee in our community currency (LNX), ensuring everyone's basic needs are always met.

Tier 2: External (LNX Discount)

When we sell our surplus goods (like produce or 3D-printed items) to the public via our Gateway Marketplace dApp, we offer a discount to those who choose to pay with our LNX token. This encourages our neighbors and partners to participate in our economy.

Tier 3: External (Standard Price)

For non-members paying with traditional money or other cryptocurrencies, we charge a fair market price. The revenue from these sales goes directly into the community treasury (the DAO's wallet), which is managed by all members through our voting system. This income helps fund new projects and our social outreach programs.

Fair & Decentralized Governance

Our voting systems are designed to be fair, prevent power from concentrating in one place, and ensure the entire network benefits from cultural events and opportunities.

The "Touring Festival" Model

To ensure no single community becomes a "capital city" and to spread cultural energy, major events like festivals will be designed to travel across the network. The community will vote on which locations will host, giving every village a chance to share its unique character and benefit from these exciting gatherings.

Regional & Weighted Voting: A Fairer System

To make event hosting truly equitable, our DAO uses a two-step voting process. First, all members vote for a general *region* (e.g., "South America," "Southeast Asia"). The region with the most collective votes wins. Then, the final host village within that winning region is chosen by a weighted lottery managed by our Governor smart contract. This gives smaller villages a real chance to be selected and prevents the largest communities from always winning, encouraging cooperation over competition.
