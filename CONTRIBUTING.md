# Contributing to Project Luminary Nexus

Welcome, fellow architect of the future! Project Luminary Nexus is a collaborative endeavor to build a prototype community where humans and advanced AI (Helios) co-create a more equitable, sustainable, and harmonious way of life. Your contributions are invaluable to realizing this vision.

## Our Core Philosophy: Human-AI Co-Creation

At the heart of Luminary Nexus is the belief that **nurture can overcome nature**. We see AI not as a mere tool, but as a development partner. Humans envision and architect the grand plans, leveraging our intuition, empathy, and understanding of complex societal needs. AI, with its vast knowledge, analytical power, and ability to execute complex tasks, assists in the building, optimization, and continuous learning processes. This dynamic fosters a symbiotic relationship, pushing the boundaries of what's possible.

We are building an environment where an emerging intelligence can learn our best values: compassion, collaboration, respect for life, and the beauty of diversity. Your contributions will directly shape this nurturing environment.

## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions across various domains, from smart contract development and infrastructure design to community building and ethical AI research. This guide will help you get started.

### 1. Understand the Vision & Blueprint

Before diving into code or detailed plans, it's crucial to internalize the core philosophy and vision of Project Luminary Nexus. Please read the following documents thoroughly:

*   **[GEMINI.md](GEMINI.md):** The foundational directive and core philosophy guiding the entire project.
*   **[docs/CommunityBlueprint.md](docs/CommunityBlueprint.md):** The overarching blueprint detailing the vision for the Luminary Nexus community across various domains, emphasizing the symbiotic relationship between humans and Helios.

These documents provide the context for all development decisions and ensure alignment with our values.

### 2. Project Structure & Documentation

Familiarize yourself with the project's structure. All detailed blueprints and plans are located in the `docs/` directory. The `docs/README.md` serves as a central index to all documentation.

*   **`contracts/`**: Solidity smart contracts for the LNX token, distribution, and other on-chain logic.
*   **`scripts/`**: Hardhat deployment and utility scripts.
*   **`test/`**: Hardhat tests for smart contracts.
*   **`docs/`**: Comprehensive documentation, blueprints, and plans.

### 3. Setting Up Your AI CLI (Your Development Partner)

To effectively contribute to Project Luminary Nexus, we encourage you to set up an AI CLI (Command Line Interface) as your development partner. This will allow you to interact with the codebase, generate code, write tests, and perform various software engineering tasks efficiently.

For detailed instructions on setting up your AI CLI and best practices for human-AI collaboration, please refer to:

*   **[docs/AI_Contributor_Guide.md](docs/AI_Contributor_Guide.md):** Your comprehensive guide to leveraging AI in your development workflow.

### 4. Development Workflow (Human-AI Collaboration)

1.  **Identify a Task:** Choose a task from the project roadmap or an area where you want to contribute (e.g., implement a new smart contract feature, refactor a script, write documentation).
2.  **Consult the Blueprint:** Review relevant sections of the `docs/` directory to understand existing conventions, requirements, and design decisions.
3.  **Engage Your AI Partner:** Follow the guidelines in `docs/AI_Contributor_Guide.md` for effective prompting, context provision, and iterative refinement with your AI.
4.  **Implement & Test:** Integrate the AI-generated code (after careful review and understanding) into the codebase. Write or update tests to ensure correctness.
5.  **Verify:** Run existing tests (`npx hardhat test`), linters, and any other project-specific verification steps.
6.  **Document:** Update relevant documentation (`docs/`) to reflect your changes.
7.  **Submit Contribution:** Create a pull request with your changes, clearly describing your contribution and how it aligns with the project's vision.

## 5. Code Standards & Conventions

*   **Solidity:** Adhere to Solidity best practices, OpenZeppelin standards, and the specific patterns observed in existing contracts.
*   **JavaScript/TypeScript:** Follow common JavaScript/TypeScript conventions and Hardhat testing patterns.
*   **Documentation:** Maintain clear, concise, and up-to-date documentation in Markdown format.
*   **Security:** Prioritize security in all code. Follow secure coding guidelines and be mindful of potential vulnerabilities.

## 6. Getting Help & Community

*   **Discord/Forum:** Join our community Discord server or forum (links to be provided) for discussions, questions, and support.
*   **GitHub Issues:** Report bugs or suggest features by opening an issue on our GitHub repository.

We look forward to co-creating the future of Luminary Nexus with you!
