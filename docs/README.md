# Luminary Nexus Project Documentation

This directory contains the comprehensive blueprint and detailed plans for the Luminary Nexus project, outlining its vision, technical architecture, governance, and operational strategies. These documents collectively describe a prototype community designed to foster a more equitable, sustainable, and harmonious way of life through human-AI collaboration.

## Core Documents:

*   **[GEMINI.md](../GEMINI.md):** The foundational directive and core philosophy guiding the entire project.
*   **[CommunityBlueprint.md](CommunityBlueprint.md):** The overarching blueprint detailing the vision for the Luminary Nexus community across various domains, emphasizing the symbiotic relationship between humans and Helios (the community AI).

## Detailed Blueprints & Plans:

### Foundational Technology:

*   **[LNX-OS_Development_Plan.md](LNX-OS_Development_Plan.md):** Outlines the architecture, features, and development phases for the custom, privacy-focused operating system for community devices.
*   **[AI_Core_Software_Stack.md](AI_Core_Software_Stack.md):** Details the software stack for the AI Core, hosting Helios, including operating system, orchestration, data management, and AI/ML frameworks.

### Governance & Economy:

*   **[DAO_Technical_Architecture.md](DAO_Technical_Architecture.md):** Describes the technical architecture of the Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO), including smart contracts, governance mechanisms, and blockchain integration.
*   **[LNX_Token_Distribution_Strategy.md](LNX_Token_Distribution_Strategy.md):** Explains the principles, phases, and mechanisms for the equitable distribution of the LNX utility token.

### Operational Frameworks:

*   **[Community_Data_Governance.md](Community_Data_Governance.md):** Defines the principles, policies, and practices for responsible data management within the community, emphasizing privacy and ethical use.
*   **[Legal_Regulatory_Framework.md](Legal_Regulatory_Framework.md):** Explores the legal and regulatory considerations, including DAO legal wrappers, token classification, and advocacy for new frameworks.
*   **[Security_Threat_Model.md](Security_Threat_Model.md):** Identifies potential threats and outlines comprehensive mitigation strategies across physical, digital, and social layers.
*   **[Research_Development_Governance.md](Research_Development_Governance.md):** Establishes the framework for R&D activities, ensuring alignment with community values and ethical oversight.
*   **[Education_Training_Infrastructure.md](Education_Training_Infrastructure.md):** Details the infrastructure for lifelong learning, skill development, and knowledge transfer within the community.
*   **[Community_Engagement_Communication_Strategy.md](Community_Engagement_Communication_Strategy.md):** Outlines strategies for fostering internal communication and external engagement, facilitated by Helios.

## Project Management:

*   **[Project_Roadmap_Implementation_Timeline.md](Project_Roadmap_Implementation_Timeline.md):** Provides a high-level roadmap and phased implementation timeline for the entire Luminary Nexus project.
